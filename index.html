An augmented-reality pop-up book

The Memory Garden

Point your phone at a Scan Here icon in the book and the memories step out of the paper: seasons turn, a window opens, seeds are planted, and a garden grows.

Scan the Book

Works in Safari (iPhone) and Chrome (Android) — no app to install.

No printed book at hand? Open the test pages on a computer screen (or print them) and scan them with your phone.

AR Mode needs camera access and works over HTTPS. For best tracking keep the page flat, well lit, and hold the phone at an angle.
